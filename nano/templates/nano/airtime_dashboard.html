Airtime Dashboard Guide
For Cashiers
  1. Sell Airtime: Click "Sell" button next to any product
  2. Enter Details: Add customer phone number and quantity
  3. Submit: Sale goes to manager for approval
  4. Track: Monitor your pending sales status
For Managers
  1. Approve Sales: Review pending cashier sales
  2. Manage Products: Add/edit airtime products
  3. Update Stock: Maintain inventory levels
  4. Handle Requests: Process cashier requests
Quick Stats
  • Total Products: All active airtime items
  • Pending Sales: Awaiting your approval
  • Requests: Product/stock change requests
  • Your Role: Current permission level
Pro Tips

💰 Profit Margins: Set selling prices higher than face value

📱 Phone Format: Use 10-15 digit numbers (no spaces)

📦 Stock Alerts: Monitor low stock levels

⚡ Fast Processing: Keep approvals prompt for customer service
